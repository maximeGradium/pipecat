- ⚠️ The Twilio, Telnyx, Plivo, Exotel, Genesys and Vonage serializers no longer resample audio; the transports do. `deserialize()` returns audio at the provider's rate, which the input transport resamples to the pipeline's, and `serialize()` drops audio that isn't at the provider's rate, the rate the output transport sends at. Code that calls one of these serializers outside a transport has to resample the audio itself.
